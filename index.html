A LITTLE ABOUT ME

ABOUT
ME.

I’m Shwet Shyam Singh Solanki, a 17-year-old student of Kendriya Vidyalaya and a national-level Judoka.

I began my Judo journey in 2024, and the sport has become an important part of my life — teaching me discipline, focus, resilience and the confidence to keep improving.

Outside academics and Judo, I enjoy music, editing, travelling, cooking, reading stories and novels, exploring history, and watching history-based web series.

I like discovering new things, staying curious and making time for the interests that keep me creative and motivated.

01 · COURAGE

MAHARANA
PRATAP

Maharana Pratap is remembered for his determined resistance to Mughal expansion in sixteenth-century Mewar. His life became a lasting symbol of courage, resilience and loyalty to his homeland. His struggle, hardship and refusal to surrender his principles continue to inspire generations.

02 · SACRIFICE

RIFLEMAN
JASWANT
SINGH RAWAT

Rifleman Jaswant Singh Rawat of 4 Garhwal Rifles is remembered for exceptional courage during the 1962 India–China conflict at Nuranang. His dedication to duty, bravery under extreme circumstances and ultimate sacrifice made his story a lasting part of Indian military history.
